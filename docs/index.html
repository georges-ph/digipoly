Your board game's bank, on every phone.

Keep your real board, dice and tokens on the table — everyone's phone becomes their wallet instead. No accounts, no sign-up, no internet required: it all runs over your own wifi, for as long as game night lasts.

How it works

Three steps, then just play.

01

Host

Open Digipoly, tap Host, and pick or create a board — it just needs to match what's on your table: properties, prices, currency.

02

Join

Everyone on the same wifi finds the room automatically, or scans the QR code shown on the host's screen.

03

Play

Play the board game exactly as normal — just use the app any time money would change hands.

Features

Everything a physical banker does — automatically.

Built to feel like a real banking app, not a boardgame counter.
